Muster in two minutes

I

Windows

Run the installer. The first launch downloads a small offline speech model. Hold Ctrl Space and talk.

II

macOS (beta)

Open the .dmg and drag the app to Applications. The first time, allow it in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway, then grant Microphone, Accessibility and Screen Recording.

III

Stay current

Check for updates in the app installs new versions in place. Your keys, macros, captures and boards are kept.
