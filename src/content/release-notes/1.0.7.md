## Boxes land where you draw them

In the capture editor, shapes drew further from the mouse with every shape you added, so the third box was clearly off. Drawing and dragging now stay under the mouse however many notes you add.

## Small screenshots stay clear of the colour panel

A small screenshot no longer sits under the colour and style panel that opens on the left while you draw. The screenshot is placed to the right of that panel, so every part of it can be reached.
