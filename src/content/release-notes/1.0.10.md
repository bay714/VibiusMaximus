## Your model, after the 1.0.8 update

Version 1.0.8 moved to a new model format. Models in the old format, including Parakeet V3 (the default for most people), Moonshine, SenseVoice and older Canary models, no longer run and need a one-time download of their new version. The app offers this on a "Your models need an update" screen.

If Vibius Maximus started in the tray, that screen never appeared: the model seemed to vanish, the dropdown said "No models available", and the shortcut did nothing. Now the window opens on startup when there's no model to dictate with, and pressing the shortcut without one opens it too, so you can update or download a model.

Models already in the new format carry over, and future updates keep them.
