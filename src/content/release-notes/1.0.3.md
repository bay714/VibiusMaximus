## Paste the image and the text with Alt+V

Some chat apps only take the image when you paste a copied capture. Now, after **Alt+C**, press **Alt+V** in any app within five minutes: it pastes the image, then your text. After that one paste, Alt+V goes back to normal.

## Pins on the board

The board has the same **Pin** tool (**Alt+`**) and **# Number shapes** as the capture editor. Each number gets a note under the canvas, and the notes are saved with the board.

On the board, **Alt+C** copies the whole board plus the text, and **Alt+V** pastes each image and then the text.
