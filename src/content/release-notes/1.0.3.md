## Paste the image and the text with Alt+V

Some chat apps only take the image when you paste a copied capture. Now, after **Alt+C**, press **Alt+V** in any app within five minutes: it pastes the image, then your text. After that one paste, Alt+V goes back to normal.

## Pins on the board

The board has the same **Pin** tool (**Alt+`**) and **# Number shapes** as the capture editor. Each number gets a note under the canvas, and the notes are saved with the board.

On the board, **Alt+C** copies the whole board plus the text, and **Alt+V** pastes each image and then the text.

## New hotkeys, all changeable

- **Alt+S** captures and **Alt+B** opens the board (were Alt+Shift+S and Alt+Shift+B).
- **Alt+D** runs AI clean-up (was Ctrl+K), and **Alt+N** turns Number shapes on or off.
- The new **Hotkeys** page in Settings lists every key, and you can change any of them.

## Save and reopen boards

Every board is kept. Click **☰** (the board's name) in the board's bar to see all your boards with a preview: open an old one, rename it, delete it, or start a **New board**. Your current board becomes "Board 1".

The tray icon is now the red shield, and its tooltip says Vibius Maximus.

New installs get two new starter macros: **Research first** (Alt+3) and **Summarize** (Alt+4). Your existing macros are kept; add these yourself in **Macros** if you'd like them.
