## Five workflow macros

The starter macros now follow a full workflow, in order, written from published prompting best practice:

- **Alt+1 Research**: primary sources first, the answer up front, a source for each finding, fact kept separate from guesswork, a worked example, and what to double-check.
- **Alt+2 Summarize**: a one-line bottom line, then each topic with background, status and next step, then the decisions needed from you.
- **Alt+3 Plan**: restates the goal and what "done" means, asks up to 3 questions if anything is unclear, then gives files, steps, checks and risks. No code until you say OK.
- **Alt+4 Scope**: a complete spec a builder can follow without guessing: goal and non-goals, testable acceptance criteria, constraints, small tasks with their own checks, review gates, risks and assumptions.
- **Alt+5 Execute**: the AI acts as orchestrator. It briefs each builder, runs work in parallel where it can, has every result reviewed against its criteria with evidence, and reports back.

Starter macros you never edited are upgraded automatically, and Execute is added on Alt+5 if that key is free. Macros you changed or made yourself stay as they are.
