## Better starter macros

The four starter macros were rewritten from published prompting best practice:

- **Alt+1 Plan first**: restates your goal, asks up to 3 questions if something is unclear, then lists files, steps, risks and how to check it works. No code until you say OK.
- **Alt+2 Pressure test**: reviews the last answer like a skeptical senior reviewer, with assumptions, a pre-mortem, missed edge cases and a confidence level, then gives a corrected version.
- **Alt+3 Research**: primary sources first, the answer up front, sources for each finding, fact kept separate from guesswork, a worked example, and what to double-check.
- **Alt+4 Summarize**: a one-line bottom line, then each topic with background, status and next step, then the decisions needed from you.

If you never edited your starter macros, they're upgraded automatically. Macros you changed or created yourself stay as they are.
