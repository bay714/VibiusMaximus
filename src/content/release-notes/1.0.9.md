## Opens reliably

Opening Vibius Maximus a second time while it was still starting, for example with a double-click, could freeze it. No window or tray icon appeared, and every later attempt to open it got stuck too. This is fixed: opening it again while it starts now just shows the window once it's ready.

If startup ever stops making progress for another reason, Vibius Maximus now restarts itself after a minute instead of staying stuck.
