## Now on Mac (beta)

VibiusMaximus now has a Mac version: `.dmg` downloads for Apple Silicon (M1 and later) and Intel Macs.

- Capture, board and macros work as on Windows. Alt is the Option key (⌥), and key hints show Mac symbols.
- Send pastes back into the app you were in. Alt+C copies the image and the text together.
- The first time you capture, macOS asks for Screen Recording permission. Quit and reopen the app after allowing it.
- The Mac app isn't notarized by Apple yet. The first time you open it, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click **Open Anyway**.

Windows: no changes in how anything works.
