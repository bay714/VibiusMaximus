## A new look: Legion

Vibius Maximus now has its own identity: a legionary shield in Pompeian red and bronze, Roman lettering for headings, and a clean, modern layout in both light and dark mode. Every section has its own icon, in the sidebar and at the top of the page.

## Easier pins

- **Pin** lives in the capture canvas (top right), or press **Alt+`**. One click drops a numbered marker, then you're back to normal.
- Turn on **# Number shapes** and every box, circle, arrow or drawing you make gets the next number and its own note.
- Numbers are ordinary objects: drag to move, **Delete** or the note's **×** to remove, **Ctrl+Z** to undo.

## Safer sending

- **Enter** no longer sends by accident. Press **Ctrl+Enter** to send, and the button shows where it goes (for example "Send to Chrome").
- **Alt+C** copies the image and all your text together, so one Ctrl+V in the chat pastes both.
- Double-click a capture in **Captures** to open it in the editor again.
