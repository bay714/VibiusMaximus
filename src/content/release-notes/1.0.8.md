## Restart when dictation stops responding

After a long session, dictation can stop working: the shortcut does nothing, it gets stuck on Transcribing, or nothing gets typed. **Restart Vibius Maximus** in the tray menu, or under Settings → General, restarts the app in a couple of seconds and clears all of these.

## Steadier over long sessions

This release brings in the latest improvements from Handy, the open-source app Vibius Maximus is built on. Transcription now runs on its own, so a model that hangs or crashes no longer freezes the whole app. While the model loads it now says so, instead of looking stuck on Transcribing. The cancel shortcut no longer stays switched on after a short recording.

The first dictation after starting the app can take a moment longer while transcription starts up.

## Paste again

If a dictation or capture went into the wrong window, click where it should go and press **Alt+Shift+V** to paste it again. After each send, a small note at the bottom of the screen says which app it went to. Turn the note off under Settings → Capture. Your last 25 dictations are also kept in History, up from 5.

## Send to another window

Under the Send button in the capture editor, **Send to another window** lists your open windows. Pick one to send the capture there instead. This also works for a reopened capture whose original window has closed.

## Old captures are cleared out

Captures older than 30 days are now deleted, so screenshots don't pile up. Starred captures are always kept. Choose 7 days, 90 days or forever under Settings → Capture.

## Dictate straight into a new number

When you draw a numbered box, the cursor now goes straight into that number's note, so you can press Ctrl+Space right away without clicking the note first.
