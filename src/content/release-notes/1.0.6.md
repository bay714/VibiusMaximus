## Dictate straight onto the board

Ctrl+Space now works on the board the way it does in the capture editor:

- **Typing in a pin's note:** the words go there, at the cursor.
- **After clicking the canvas:** the words still go to the note of the pin you placed or selected last.
- **Editing a text box on the canvas:** the words go into that text box.
- **No pins yet:** the words become a new text box in the middle of the board.
